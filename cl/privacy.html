Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Introduction

EZer ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address and name when you create an account
  • Financial Data: Expense and income entries you manually add to the app
  • Goals Data: Savings goals and fund allocations you create
  • Budget Data: Budget categories and limits you set
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app to improve our service

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the EZer service
  • Display your financial data within the app
  • Generate analytics and insights about your spending
  • Sync your data across devices (if you enable cloud backup)
  • Send important service notifications

Data Storage & Security

Your financial data is stored locally on your device by default. If you enable cloud backup:

  • Data is encrypted with AES-256 encryption before upload
  • We use secure cloud infrastructure (Google Firebase)
  • Data is encrypted both in transit and at rest
  • Data on your phone is encrypted (AES-256) and shared household items are end-to-end encrypted. Some details are stored on our servers in a form we could read: household and member names and roles, dates of shared items, AI parsing results (unless you turn the cache off) and the backup encryption key.

Crash Reporting

We collect anonymous crash reports and non-fatal error logs under our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable (GDPR Article 6(1)(f); equivalent legal bases under DPDPA and similar frameworks). These reports help us identify and fix bugs quickly. Reports include:

  • Crash stack traces and error context
  • Device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Approximate region (derived from IP; the IP itself is not stored)

We never include your transactions, balances, names, or other personal information in crash reports. You can disable crash reporting at any time in Settings → Privacy → Crash Reporting.

What We Don't Do

  • We DO NOT sell your data to third parties
  • We DO NOT share your data with advertisers
  • We DO NOT access your bank accounts directly
  • We DO NOT store your bank passwords
  • We DO NOT read SMS messages or store SMS content on servers

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all data from within the app
  • Export: Export your data from the app

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@ezerapp.com

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app.

Children's Privacy

EZer is intended for adults and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

AI Statement Parsing (EZer Plus, optional)

AI statement parsing turns a bank or credit card statement that you choose (PDF, image or CSV) into transactions. It is off until you give consent in the app, and you can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy Dashboard. All other features work without it.

The app sends the statement to our server, which sends its content, with the country and currency codes, statement type and your category names, to our AI provider: Anthropic (Claude), in the United States. Your name, email and account ID are not sent to Anthropic, and no other AI provider receives your statement. Anthropic may keep what it receives for a limited period (for example, up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring.

The statement file is not stored on our servers. The parsed result is kept in our database, linked to your account, so the same statement is not parsed and charged again; you can turn this off in Settings → Security Settings, and Delete All Data removes it.

Household Sharing (EZer Plus, optional, Android)

In the Android app, up to 6 people can share transactions, budgets, goals and bills, split bills and settle up as a household. Every member needs EZer Plus and must sign in with Google or Microsoft, and you join only through an invite from a member. Once you join, what you record is shared with the household by default; in Settings → Household you can exclude categories, hide accounts or stop sharing single items. Only the other members of your household see your shared items.

Shared items are end-to-end encrypted: amounts, descriptions, merchants, notes, categories and other details are encrypted on your phone (AES-256-GCM) with a household key that never reaches our servers in readable form, so we cannot read them. To run the household, our server stores the household name, each member's name, role and account ID, and the date, type and status of each shared item, in Google Cloud Firestore in India (Mumbai, asia-south1); the household server functions run in the same region. When you leave you can delete the items you shared, the owner can delete the household, and Delete All Data deletes your shared items and any household you own.

International Data Transfers

Data held on our servers is processed by Google Cloud (Firebase). Our database is in India (Mumbai, asia-south1). Household sharing functions run in India (asia-south1); AI statement parsing, subscription and purchase functions run in the United States (us-central1). AI statement parsing also sends statement content to Anthropic in the United States. Where your data is transferred outside your country, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and, for UK users, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum) included in Google's Cloud Data Processing Addendum and Anthropic's Data Processing Addendum. You can ask for a copy at privacy@ezerapp.com.
