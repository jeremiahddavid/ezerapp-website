Data Deletion Request

Request deletion of your personal data

Option 1: Delete Data In-App

Recommended

You can delete all your data directly from the EZer app:

  1. 1 Open EZer app on your device
  2. 2 Go to Settings → Delete Account, or Settings → Privacy Dashboard → Delete All Data
  3. 3 Review what will be deleted
  4. 4 Tap "Delete All Data"
  5. 5 Type DELETE to confirm

Important

Deleting your data in-app is immediate and permanent. This action cannot be undone. The app first asks our server to delete your server-side data, then wipes the EZer database on your device. A few local settings (for example the app-lock PIN and cached sign-in tokens) may remain on the device until you uninstall the app.

What Gets Deleted

On your device:

  • ✓ All financial transactions and records
  • ✓ Account information
  • ✓ Budget and goal data
  • ✓ Scanned receipts and images
  • ✓ App preferences and settings
  • ✓ Local encrypted database

On our servers:

  • ✓ Your anonymous account (and the Google or Microsoft sign-in linked to it)
  • ✓ Parsed statements (parse cache), parse jobs and parse history
  • ✓ Credit wallet and credit history, and your subscription record
  • ✓ Privacy choice records (a pseudonymised copy is archived for 7 years, as proof of consent)
  • ✓ AI-parse problem reports and receipt scan feedback you sent, including images
  • ✓ Your cloud-backup encryption key

NOT Deleted Automatically

  • ✗ Backup files already in your own cloud storage account
  • ✗ Exported files saved to your device
  • ✗ Statement copies kept by the AI provider for their own retention period (typically up to 30 days)
  • ✗ Crash and analytics data kept by Firebase under its retention settings

Cloud backup (Google Drive / OneDrive) is part of EZer Plus where available. Backup files stay in your cloud account, but they cannot be restored after deletion because the encryption key is deleted with your data. Delete them from the EZer app folder of your cloud storage account if you want them gone too.

Option 2: Request via Email

If you cannot access the app or need assistance, request deletion via email:

To: privacy@ezerapp.com

Subject: Data Deletion Request

Include the following information:

  • If you signed in: the Google or Microsoft email address you signed in with
  • If you never signed in, your EZer account is anonymous and has no name or email, so we cannot find it from an email request. Please use Delete All Data in the app (Option 1).
  • Confirmation that you want to delete all data

Frequently Asked Questions

Will deleting data in-app also delete my cloud backups?

No. Backup files are stored in your own cloud storage account and must be deleted there. After Delete All Data they can no longer be restored, because the encryption key is deleted with your data.

Can I recover my data after deletion?

No. Data deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Backup files made before the deletion cannot be restored either, because the backup encryption key is deleted with your data.

What happens if I just uninstall the app?

Uninstalling removes the data on your phone only. Your server-side data (see "On our servers" above) remains until you delete it in the app or by email. Backups in your own cloud account remain and can be restored after reinstalling if you sign in with the same account and have not deleted your data.

Does EZer keep any data on its servers after I delete my account?

Your everyday data is stored on your device. If you use AI statement parsing, the parsed transactions are stored on our servers until you delete them (or you can switch caching off in Security Settings with "Don't cache my statements on our servers"); there is no automatic expiry. After Delete All Data we keep only a pseudonymised consent record for 7 years, as the law requires, and aggregate crash and analytics data that cannot be linked back to you.

How do I export my data before deleting?

Before deleting, go to Settings → Privacy Dashboard → Export my data. You receive one ZIP with CSV and JSON files. Save it to your device; exported files are not encrypted.
