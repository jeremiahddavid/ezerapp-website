What is household sharing?

Household sharing lets you manage money together with the people you live with — a spouse, partner, kids, parents, or roommates — without losing the privacy of your personal finances.

Share a grocery budget, split the electricity bill, give your teen an allowance, or save together toward a family holiday. Anything you don’t explicitly share stays personal and stays on your device.

How does it work?

End-to-end encrypted

Shared data is encrypted on your device before it leaves. EZer can’t read it on our servers.

Opt-in only

Nothing is shared automatically. You pick which budgets, goals, and transactions become Shared.

Personal stays personal

Your private transactions, paychecks, and accounts stay on your device, invisible to everyone else.

What gets shared: Items you tag as Shared — selected budgets, savings goals, bills, allowances, chores, and split transactions.
What stays personal: All other transactions, accounts, your salary, your personal goals, and your spending history.

Setting up your household

It takes less than two minutes.

  1. 1

    Open Household from the home screen

    Tap the Household tab (or go to Settings → Household). Choose Create Household.

  2. 2

    Name your household and pick a currency

    Pick anything — “The Smiths,” “Apt 4B,” “Mom & Dad.” The household currency is what shared budgets and bills are shown in.

  3. 3

    Invite your family

    Generate an invite code and share it through any messaging app. They install EZer, paste the code, and they’re in.

Inviting family members

Generating an invite code

  1. Go to Household → Members → Invite.
  2. Pick the role you want the new member to have (you can change it later).
  3. Tap Generate code. EZer creates a short, one-time code that expires after 7 days.
  4. Tap Share to send via WhatsApp, iMessage, email, or any other app.

Accepting an invite

  1. Install EZer (Android or iOS) and finish the quick setup.
  2. Go to Household → Join Household.
  3. Paste the invite code and confirm. You’re in.
Tip: Invite codes are one-time use. If you need to invite multiple people, generate a fresh code for each — that way you can give each one a different role.

Roles explained

Every household member has a role that controls what they can do. The household creator is the Owner by default and can change anyone’s role at any time.

Owner

Full control. Can invite, remove, and change anyone’s role. Can delete the household. Every household needs exactly one Owner.

Adult

Can add shared transactions, create shared budgets and goals, split bills, set allowances, and assign chores. Can’t remove other members.

Teen

Can log their own spending, complete chores, request reimbursements, and see their allowance. Doesn’t see other members’ transactions.

Kid

Kid-friendly view focused on their piggy bank, allowance, and assigned chores. Spending is logged by an Adult or Owner.

Viewer

Read-only access to shared budgets and totals. Good for an accountant, parent helping out, or extended family who needs to see the numbers but not change anything.

Common tasks

Split a bill

Add the transaction, tap Split, choose who’s on it, and pick an equal split or custom amounts. Everyone sees the totals owed in their Household → Activity tab.

Request a reimbursement

On any personal transaction, tap Request reimbursement, pick the household member who owes you, and add a note. They get a notification and can mark it Paid from their side.

Set up an allowance

Go to Household → Allowances → New. Pick a recipient (Teen or Kid), the amount, and the schedule (weekly, biweekly, monthly). EZer automatically credits their piggy bank on schedule.

Assign a chore

In Household → Chores, tap +, give it a name, set a reward amount, choose who’s responsible, and pick a due date. The reward is auto-added to their piggy bank when an Adult marks it complete.

Unlock a piggy bank

Piggy banks belong to Teens and Kids. The kid can request an unlock from their screen; an Adult or Owner gets a notification to approve or decline. Approved unlocks become spendable funds.

Privacy & security

  • • End-to-end encryption. Shared data is encrypted with a household key that only members hold. EZer servers store ciphertext only.
  • • Key rotation. Whenever a member is added or removed, the household key is automatically rotated so old members can never decrypt new data.
  • • Leaving the household. You take your personal data with you. Shared items stay with the household, but your device stops syncing them and your local cache is wiped.
  • • Being removed. Works the same as leaving: your access to shared content ends, the key rotates, and your personal data is untouched.
  • • No analytics on financial data. EZer never logs the amount, merchant, or category of any transaction — shared or personal.

FAQ

Is household sharing free?

You can create a household and invite up to 2 members on the free plan. EZer Plus removes the member limit and unlocks unlimited shared budgets and goals.

Can my partner see my salary?

Not unless you mark it as Shared. Income transactions stay personal by default. The only thing other members see is what you explicitly contribute to a shared budget or goal.

What happens if I change phones?

Reinstall EZer, restore your backup, and you’re back in your household. Make sure backup is on in Settings → Backup before switching devices.

Can I be in more than one household?

Right now each EZer user belongs to one household at a time. You can leave one and join another, but not be in two at once.

What if a kid doesn’t have a phone?

You can create a Kid “managed” member that doesn’t need their own device — an Adult logs spending and chore completions on their behalf, and the kid’s allowance and piggy bank still track normally.

How do I change someone’s role?

Only the Owner can. Go to Household → Members, tap a member, and pick a new role. They’ll be notified.

Can I transfer ownership?

Yes. In Household → Members, promote an Adult to Owner. The previous Owner automatically becomes an Adult. Useful if you’re leaving the household but want it to keep running.

What happens to the household if the Owner leaves?

Before leaving, the Owner has to transfer ownership to another Adult. If the Owner is the only member, leaving deletes the household.

Do household members see each other’s passwords or biometrics?

No. Every member uses their own EZer install with their own app lock, PIN, or biometric. Nothing about your authentication is shared.

Can a Viewer see how much I personally spent?

Only the totals for shared budgets and goals. They don’t see individual personal transactions from any member.

Troubleshooting

“Invite code invalid or expired”

Invite codes expire after 7 days and are one-time-use. Ask the Owner to generate a fresh code. Double-check there are no extra spaces when pasting.

Shared budgets aren’t updating

Pull down to refresh on the Household tab. If it still doesn’t sync, check your internet connection — shared data needs network access to sync (personal data doesn’t). Reopen the app if needed.

A member shows as “Pending” forever

They haven’t opened the invite yet. Resend the code, or ask them to confirm they pasted it in Household → Join Household (not in a search field).

I can’t see the Household tab

Make sure you’re on the latest version of EZer. On very old versions Household may not be available — update via Google Play or the App Store.

Removed a member by mistake

Just re-invite them. They’ll rejoin the same household, but they’ll need to be re-added to any specific shared budgets they were on. (Past activity totals remain.)
